04 · Rules of use

DOOne italic accent per headline. Use .accent on a single phrase, never two.
DOOne mauve element per slide. Mauve (#C77DFF) is the focal accent — KPI, badge, or pill dot.
DOKeep chrome consistent. Brand top-left, page counter bottom-right, on every slide.
DOLimit body text to 2 sentences per block. Long copy belongs in speaker notes.
DON'TUse gradients, blurs, drop-shadows, or pseudo-element decoration. Flat fills only.
DON'TMix mauve and plum on the same element. Plum is structural; mauve is the highlight.
DON'TAdd decorative emoji or icons. Use type, rules, and KPI numbers as visual rhythm.
DON'TReplace Instrument Serif with another italic. The italic accent is the brand signature.
